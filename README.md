I'm **Silas André**, a Brazilian multidisciplinary professional working at the intersection of **growth, design and technology**.

I combine strategic thinking, creative direction and software development to turn ideas into digital experiences, build products and explore new opportunities.

I'm interested in understanding not only how things are built, but also **why they work, who they serve and how they grow.**

My approach is simple: strategy gives direction, design creates meaning, and technology makes things happen.
